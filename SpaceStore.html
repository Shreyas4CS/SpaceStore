Why a shop?

A supermarket is a data science lab

A supermarket keeps track of hundreds of things: how much rice is left, what sold today, which soap needs restocking before Monday. That is data, and the shopkeeper uses it to make decisions every hour.

In Space Store your team becomes the data team for a shop. You build the inventory from scratch, organise it so a computer can understand it, and turn it into charts that answer real questions.

Good data, good decisions 🛒
📝Every item is a recordName, unit, quantity and price make one row of data.
🔑IDs stop mix-upsTwo kinds of rice? A unique ID tells them apart.
📊Charts answer fastOne glance shows the bestseller and the empty shelf.
🔔Rules act for youWhen stock drops below your level, the app reminds you.
The 3 stages

Collect. Organise. Visualise. Sell.

Real data scientists follow these same steps. You complete each one in Admin mode, then open the shop.

📝
stage one

Data collection

Create every item: its name, unit (kg, g, pcs), quantity and price.

🗂️
stage two

Data organisation

Give each item a unique ID, sort it into categories, set restock levels and build the table.

📊
stage three

Data visualisation

Snap blocks together to make formulas, a bar chart of sales and a pie chart of stock.

🛍️
then

Open the shop

Shoppers buy, pay at checkout, and your stock and charts update by themselves.

Two modes

The shopkeeper builds. The shopper buys.

Admin mode · password protected

Behind the counter

Set up the shop the first time through the three data stages. After that, add new products in one form and keep an eye on alerts.

  • 1Collect, organise and visualise your inventory
  • 2See live numbers, charts and restock alerts
  • 3Add products with ID, category and price in seconds
🧑‍💼
Shop mode · open to everyone

In front of the counter

Shoppers browse by category, fill a cart and pay through a realistic checkout. Every purchase reduces the stock in Admin mode.

  • 1Browse products with prices and stock left
  • 2Pay by UPI, card, net banking or wallet (demo)
  • 3Sold-out items show Out of stock instantly
